EXERCISE-2 – 15 RESEARCH-PAPER VISUALIZATION REDESIGNS

This package contains:
- 3 compact Exercise-2 DOCX record files matching the uploaded reference's heading structure.
- 1 companion DOCX containing the complete research-paper selection, links, visualization analyses,
  educational reconstructions, improved designs, before/after tables, Python code, analysis and results.
- 15 improved PNG visualizations.
- 15 educational poor/reconstruction PNG visualizations.
- 15 individual Python source files and one combined runner.
- Research bibliography, paper-download verification and selection table.

SOURCE NOTE:
The uploaded reference document uses the headings EXERCISE-2, AIM, PROCEDURE, CODE, RESULT,
followed by PERFORMANCE (25), VIVA (10), RECORD (15), TOTAL (50). The three record DOCX files
retain this structure. The detailed research content requested by the student is placed in the
separate companion DOCX.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY:
Original research figures are identified by paper and figure number. Where exact numerical values
were not available in accessible text/tables, the redesign is explicitly treated as an educational
or conceptual reconstruction and does not claim to reproduce the paper's empirical results.
The improved figures are original Python-generated educational designs.

PDF STATUS:
The selected IEEE Xplore and ScienceDirect records were checked on the publisher pages for
open-access/free-PDF availability at the time of preparation. Direct PDF URLs are included in
the companion document and bibliography data.
